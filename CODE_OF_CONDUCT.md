# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small utility that blacks out monitors, not somewhere anyone needs to
have a hard time.

## What that means here

- Assume the person on the other end is trying to help, including when they are reporting that your
  favourite feature is broken.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or unwelcome attention of any kind. No slurs.
- Someone who says a topic is finished is entitled to have it be finished.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately through
[GitHub's advisory form](https://github.com/zedle/BlackScreens/security/advisories/new), which
reaches the maintainer and nobody else, or by any other private channel you already have.

Reports are read by the maintainer, who may edit or delete comments, close issues, or block accounts.
Anything serious enough to need action gets it, without the reporter having to argue for it.
